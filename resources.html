Resource Library

Curated reading, research, and advocacy opportunities to support your growth. Explore our collection of books, articles, scholarly work, and advocacy resources on women's leadership, workplace equity, and personal empowerment.

Book

Lean In

by Sheryl Sandberg

A powerful exploration of women, work, and the will to lead, offering practical advice on how women can overcome barriers to reach their full potential in the workplace and beyond.

Book

Becoming

by Michelle Obama

An intimate and inspiring memoir tracing Michelle Obama's journey from the South Side of Chicago to the White House, exploring the experiences that shaped her and the importance of finding your own voice.

Book

Invisible Women: Exposing Data Bias in a World Designed for Men

by Caroline Criado Perez

A groundbreaking investigation into how the gender data gap affects women's lives, from workplace design to medical research, revealing the hidden biases embedded in systems built without women in mind.

Book

Career and Family

by Claudia Goldin

From the Nobel Prize-winning economist, a landmark study tracing how generations of women have navigated the tension between career ambitions and family life, and why true equity remains elusive.

Book

Women Don't Ask

by Linda Babcock & Sara Laschever

A pioneering look at why women often don't negotiate for themselves and how this reluctance contributes to the gender gap in pay, advancement, and opportunities.

Book

Men and Women of the Corporation

by Rosabeth Moss Kanter

A classic study of how corporate structures shape careers and self-images, revealing how the distribution of power and opportunity within organizations affects women's advancement.

Book

The Confidence Code

by Katty Kay & Claire Shipman

An exploration of the science and art of self-assurance, revealing what confidence really is, why women often lack it, and how they can develop it to succeed in every aspect of life.

Article

The Gender Pay Gap: What It Is and Why It Persists

Various Authors

An in-depth overview of wage inequality between genders. This article examines the causes, impacts, and potential solutions to closing the gender pay gap in modern workplaces across industries.

Article

How Young Women Are Redefining Leadership

Various Authors

A look at emerging leadership styles and approaches among young women professionals. Discover how this generation is challenging traditional models and bringing fresh perspectives to leadership roles.

Research

Women in the Workplace 2025

McKinsey & LeanIn.Org

The annual study on the state of women in corporate America. This comprehensive research examines hiring, promotion, attrition, and representation of women across levels and industries.
