2026 National High School Women's Conference

Conference Overview

On October 4, 2026, HERizon brought together more than 100 students from over 35 schools for its inaugural National High School Women's Conference. Through interactive workshops, panel discussions, and conversations with accomplished women leaders, attendees explored topics including education, careers, negotiation, and mental wellness, leaving with new perspectives, practical advice, and meaningful connections.
