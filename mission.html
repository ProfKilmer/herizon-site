Our Mission

Empower every young woman to reach her full potential.

HERizon exists to help young women understand and navigate their education, careers, and lives. We recognize that while access to opportunity and gender gaps have improved over the past decades, meaningful barriers still persist, ranging from unequal access to mentorship, differences in advancement opportunities and negotiated outcomes, pressures to maintain appearances, tradeoffs between career and family, and both conscious and implicit biases.

By providing access to information, resources, networks, and actionable insights, HERizon aspires to make these systems more transparent and provide tools to help navigate these barriers. Our goal is to help young women make informed decisions and approach their present and future with clarity and confidence.

Our Three Pillars

Community

We bring young women together to share experiences, build relationships, and support one another. Through our conference, discussion boards, and online community, we create spaces where women can find mentors, allies, and peers who understand the challenges they face.

Knowledge

We provide access to information, research, and curated resources that help young women understand the barriers they may face and the opportunities available to them. By making these systems more transparent, we help women make informed decisions about their education, careers, and lives.

Empowerment

We equip young women with the tools, skills, and confidence to advocate for themselves and navigate obstacles. From negotiation strategies to leadership development, we provide actionable insights that help women approach their present and future with clarity and confidence.
